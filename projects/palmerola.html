Honduras

Palmerola

Urban Design — Master Plan, with Gehl

Location Honduras · Scope Master Plan · Status In development

Castillo Arquitectos joined forces with Gehl, the renowned Danish urban design consultancy founded by Jan Gehl, to develop the Palmerola Master Plan: a new mixed-use urban district in the heart of Honduras.

The collaboration paired Gehl’s focus on human scale and public space with Castillo Arquitectos’ deep knowledge of the local context, regulations, and development dynamics of the region, shaping a place where everyday life is the starting point of the design.

Palmerola is a one-of-a-kind development pole in the region, set alongside Palmerola International Airport, Honduras’ new gateway to the world. That strategic location makes the project a meeting point between the local and the global — built for the people who live there and for those arriving from anywhere in the world.

Las Catalinas, Guanacaste, Costa Rica

Casa Virginia

Custom House · 400 m²

Location Las Catalinas, Guanacaste, Costa Rica · Scope Custom Residence · Area 400 m² · Status Built

Casa Virginia is a single-family residence in Las Catalinas conceived as a response to its specific site, with terraces and outdoor spaces that extend domestic life toward the community’s coastal landscape.

Situated on a steep site in Las Catalinas’ Beach Town, it rises as a gleaming white volume under the Costa Rican sun. Protective balconies and roofed terraces frame views from every level to the Pacific.

The building’s vertical presence and restrained white facade give it a quiet monumentality — a landmark within the community without demanding attention.

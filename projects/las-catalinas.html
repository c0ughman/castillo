Las Catalinas

Architecture — Mixed-Use within a Planned Community

Location Guanacaste, Costa Rica · Scope Mixed-Use Architecture · Status Ongoing
The Town

Las Catalinas is a car-free coastal town on the Pacific coast of Guanacaste, Costa Rica, designed under the principles of New Urbanism. Castillo Arquitectos takes part in the community’s ongoing development of buildings, contributing to both the residential and mixed-use architecture that gives it form.

Architecture

Plaza del Mercado is a mixed-use market building that frames a civic plaza, with retail at ground level and flats above. It responds to the town’s Mediterranean and Latin American colonial architectural language while expressing its own character.

The result is a walkable community where daily life unfolds among plazas, paths, and shops, with the coastal landscape as a constant reference.

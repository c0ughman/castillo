Coatepeque, Guatemala

Nueva Miramar

Urban Design — Master Plan + Architecture

Location Coatepeque, Guatemala · Scope Master Plan + Architecture · Area 1,000+ hectares · Status Phase 1 in design
Master Plan

Nueva Miramar is a 1,000+ hectare master plan created by Gehl Architects, an internationally acclaimed Danish urban design firm, in collaboration with Castillo Arquitectos, combining global urban design expertise with Castillo Arquitectos’ deep knowledge of the Guatemalan market, culture, and context.

Located in Coatepeque, the plan transforms a predominantly agricultural landscape into a complete urban community, organized around public space, with a mix of residential and commercial uses and the site’s landscape as the design’s starting point.

Architecture

It’s one of the most ambitious urban development projects currently underway in Guatemala, setting a new standard for people-centered planning in Central America. Castillo Arquitectos is also developing the architecture for its first phase: a commercial district, a natural park, and a sports park.

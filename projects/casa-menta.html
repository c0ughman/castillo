Las Catalinas, Guanacaste, Costa Rica

Casa Menta

Custom House · 400 m²

Location Las Catalinas, Guanacaste, Costa Rica · Scope Custom Residence · Area 400 m² · Status Built

Casa Menta is a single-family residence in Las Catalinas designed from its particular siting, with spaces open to the exterior and a materiality meant to age with dignity within the community’s architectural language.

Green — from the mint-tea colour of its walls to the unruly vines in its private garden — is integral to this corner house arranged around a central courtyard. Soaring levels permit extraordinary views to the turquoise waters of Costa Rica’s Pacific coast.

The house combines the discipline of the courtyard plan with a vertical ambition that makes the most of its elevated position in the town.

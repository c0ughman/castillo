Who We Are

A studio rooted in place, working at every scale.

A multidisciplinary design studio based in Guatemala City, working across Central America and beyond.

2019

Prix Versailles — Special Mention

Forbes Centroamérica — Top Creatives

Castillo Arquitectos is a team of architects, urban designers, and creatives, driven by the opportunity to design places that endure and nurture a true sense of community.

With over two decades of experience, we’ve earned a reputation for delivering holistic projects that stay true to the unique character of each place and the evolving needs of people and context. Our personalized approach places clients — and the communities we serve — at the heart of every decision.

Rooted in Central America and connected to the world, we integrate deep knowledge of regional regulations, cultures, and markets with a global perspective, in long-standing collaborations with leading developers, consultants, and institutions.

At our core, we believe design is legacy — one that connects people, elevates everyday life, and creates a lasting, positive impact for future generations.

Architecture begins long before the first line is drawn — in conversation, in observation, in a long look at the place itself.

Founder & Director

Eduardo Castillo Cortés

Architect · Urban Designer

Guided by Eduardo Castillo Cortés — an accomplished designer with more than 20 years of international, multidisciplinary experience — the team unites strategic thinkers and creative minds to shape places that inspire, endure, and enrich the human experience.

He founded the studio in 2004, after training and collaborating with leading firms in the United States. His work in urban design and architecture has been recognised internationally, and he has collaborated with institutions and developers throughout the Americas.

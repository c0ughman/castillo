Who We Are

A studio rooted in place, working at every scale.

A multidisciplinary design studio based in Guatemala City, working across Central America and beyond.

2019

Prix Versailles — Special Mention

2020

Forbes Centroamérica — Top Creatives

Castillo Arquitectos is a multidisciplinary design studio based in Guatemala City. For more than two decades, the firm has designed master plans, urban communities, mixed-use buildings, and custom homes across Central America — and collaborated on projects in more than a dozen countries worldwide.

The studio’s work spans scales: from the overall structure of a new town to the proportion of a single window. What connects them is a consistent belief that the best design emerges from a deep understanding of place, a genuine commitment to the people who will inhabit it, and a craft ethic that carries through every decision.

Castillo Arquitectos works with a selective group of clients — developers, institutions, and private individuals — who share that belief. The firm engages as a true partner: present from the first conversation to the finished building.

Architecture begins long before the first line is drawn — in conversation, in observation, in a long look at the place itself.

Founder & Director

Eduardo Castillo Cortés

Architect · Urban Designer

Eduardo Castillo Cortés founded the studio in 2004, after training and collaborating with leading firms in the United States. His work in urban design and architecture has been recognised internationally, and he has collaborated with institutions and developers throughout the Americas. Eduardo defines the studio’s design vision and takes an active part in every project, alongside a core team committed to the same standard of work.

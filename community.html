Master Planning & Community Design

The studio’s primary service.

Master Planning & Community Design is the studio’s primary service. We create master plans that balance beauty, functionality, and long-term sustainability. From new neighborhoods to urban development projects, our community designs prioritize walkability, social connection, and a strong sense of place.

Beyond planning, we design the architecture of the buildings that give shape to a community — from residential typologies to mixed-use and civic architecture — with special emphasis on how the built environment strengthens identity and belonging. Urban design and architecture are not separate services: they are two phases of the same integrated practice.

Urban Design

Strategic plans for new communities, city districts, and resort destinations.

Castillo Arquitectos designs walkable communities, with a mix of uses, public spaces, and an identity grounded in each site’s topography and climate. The master plan defines the structure of streets, plazas, and parks that makes a life of proximity and encounter possible.

  • New town & neighbourhood master plans
  • Urban infill & regeneration
  • Planned resort & tourist communities
  • Form-based codes & development regulations
  • Participatory design charrettes
Architecture

The buildings that give communities their identity.

The studio’s architecture gives built form to the vision of each master plan and each place: mixed-use buildings, multifamily housing, and civic spaces that translate urban principles into livable experiences, with a materiality meant to age with dignity within its setting.

  • Mixed-use commercial & civic buildings
  • Club houses & community amenity buildings
  • Hospitality & resort architecture
  • Market buildings & civic plazas
  • Residential typologies within planned communities
Selected Projects

Plan and building, one project.

Each project below spans both phases — the master plan that sets the structure, and the architecture that gives it built form.

01

El Salvador

El Encanto

Master Plan + Architecture

El Salvador’s most exclusive residential and golf community, with a course designed by Perry Dye. Castillo Arquitectos led the master planning for its residential expansion and future development, through an intensive five-day participatory design process. The plan sets out a four-pillar strategy — mobility, public space, housing, and ecological landscape. The studio also led the architecture for the Casa Club renovation and expansion, and now leads new housing typologies.

02

Curridabat, Costa Rica

Ciudad del Este

Master Plan + Architecture · Phases 1–3 complete

A master plan in ongoing development east of San José, conceived as a new urban centre for a growing area that lacked public space, integrating offices, retail, hospitality, and housing around a network of streets, plazas, and parks. Its first phase challenged the predominant model of enclosed, car-oriented commercial architecture, delivering a project that generates active street life and creates genuine public space.

View project
03

Guanacaste, Costa Rica

Las Catalinas

Architecture within a Planned Community

A car-free coastal town on the Pacific coast of Guanacaste, designed under the principles of New Urbanism. Castillo Arquitectos takes part in the community’s ongoing development of buildings, contributing to both the residential and mixed-use architecture that gives it form — a walkable community where daily life unfolds among plazas, paths, and shops.

Las Catalinas, Guanacaste, Costa Rica

Los Altos Flats

Architecture — Multifamily Residential

Multifamily housing on a tropical hillside. Two residential buildings adapt to the site’s natural slope through a stepped section, giving each residence open views toward the Pacific, privacy, natural light, and cross ventilation. The architecture recedes into the landscape to enhance it.

04

Coatepeque, Guatemala

Nueva Miramar

Master Plan + Architecture · 1,000+ hectares

A 1,000+ hectare master plan created by Gehl Architects in collaboration with Castillo Arquitectos, transforming a predominantly agricultural landscape into a complete urban community organized around public space. Castillo Arquitectos is also developing the architecture for its first phase: a commercial district, a natural park, and a sports park.

05

Honduras

Palmerola

Master Plan, with Gehl

A new mixed-use urban district in the heart of Honduras, developed with Gehl, the Danish consultancy founded by Jan Gehl. Set alongside Palmerola International Airport, the project is a meeting point between the local and the global — built for the people who live there and for those arriving from anywhere in the world.

06

Panamá

Santa María Leisure District

Master Plan + Conceptual Architecture · 120,000 m²

A vision for the heart of Santa María — the centre of one of Panama’s premier luxury communities. Castillo Arquitectos designed and consolidated the district’s commercial and entertainment core, bringing together the Country Club, the Hotel, the Golf Course, the complete Village Center, and new parcels for Branded Residences.
